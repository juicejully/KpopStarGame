# 🎤 K-Pop Star Game

An indie K-Pop simulation and management game focused on the music industry, built in **Unity (C#)**. In the game, you take control of an entertainment agency, manage trainees and idols, produce music, organize world tours, and navigate the complex highs and lows of the idol world.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **💿 Full Music Production:** Create albums in *Single*, *Mini*, or *Full* formats, choose concepts, set recording days, define *Title Tracks* and B-Sides, and manage custom album covers.
* **🌍 Advanced Fan System (*Fan Buckets*):** Track fan growth divided by countries, age groups, gender, and satisfaction levels.
* **✈️ Global Tours & Shows:** Plan international tour routes, adjust ticket prices, and watch crowds fill venues based on popularity and engagement.
* **📺 Castings & Opportunities:** Your idols can be cast in dramas (Korea and overseas), movies, renowned magazine covers, or become luxury brand ambassadors (*Muse*).
* **💬 Internal Social Media (*Whisper*):** A dynamic system of fan posts, entertainment portal news, and official announcements that react in real-time to your agency's actions and achievements.
* **📈 Career & Contract Management:** Renew contracts, add or remove group members, manage project units, and handle company finances.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies & Tools

* **Engine:** Unity
* **Language:** C#
* **Architecture:** Turn/week-based management pattern (`GameDate`, `YMW` system).
